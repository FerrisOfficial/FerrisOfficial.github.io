Bachelor of Computer Science at the University of Wrocław. Thesis and coursework
focused on C++20, Python, game AI and machine learning.

Core coursework:

- Data structures and algorithms
- Multithreading and concurrency
- Performance optimisation and profiling
- Memory management
- Debugging and performance analysis
- Data processing and analysis

Alongside a substantial mathematics component — probability and statistics, discrete
mathematics, time series analysis, numerical analysis and logic.
